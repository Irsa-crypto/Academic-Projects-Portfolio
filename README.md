## Overview – Second Year

In this branch, you will find the main projects that marked my second year of the BUT program.  
These projects allowed me to deepen my knowledge in IT networks and explore advanced concepts in my specialization: Networks, Operators and Multimedia.  
Many of the skills I learned this year were later applied and reinforced during my internships in the industry.

---

## Advanced Networking & IT Infrastructure

Throughout the year, I worked on advanced network projects, including deployment of a multi-site telephony service using 3CX, Cisco Packet Tracer, and concepts like SIP trunks and DMZ. But also, designing a secure IT infrastructure, including Asterisk, Linphone, and other tools for communication services.

I also improved my website, which I presented as slides with page previews.  
It can also be consulted online via [this link](https://chandny01.github.io/Irsa-RT/).

---

## Telecommunications

I learned important concepts in telecommunications, including:

- Fiber optic manufacturing, usage, and installation, simulating multiple projects from the central office to the end-user building.  
- Using tools such as the OTDR (Optical Time Domain Reflectometer) to measure and test fiber networks.  
- Understanding electromagnetic waves and their importance in our field.  
- Practical lessons in telecom physics, including Smith charts, impedance measurements, and using precise telecom instruments.
