Here you will find the main projects that marked my first year of the BUT program. They summarize everything I learned during this year and show how I started building my skills in IT and networking. Throughout the year, I discovered the fundamentals of computer networks, including IP addressing, VLANs, NAT and address translation. By configuring network equipment for the first time, I gained a clear understanding of how real infrastructures are built and managed. I also created my first websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP, and I worked with CSV files to read, process and analyze data for the first time. I also explored the world of cybersecurity. One of my key projects was creating a complete awareness video about security best practices (from writing the script to recording and editing the entire video) a skill I already had thanks to my experience on YouTube. Another important part of this year was learning how microprocessors work. I had to understand their architecture and complete a programming script to control LEDs of ESP32. This project helped me understand how low-level instructions, registers and processor logic work and familiarize with the C++ language. All of this was a big step for me, especially considering that I came from a scientific high school background (maths, physics, chemistry and English) with no prior studies in computer science. At the same time, I managed to lead a cybersecurity club during the second half of the year, participate in theatre on Thursday evenings, and work a student job on the weekends.
